  32 (all-false/all-true/mixed/empty visibility), 38/43 (resource-limit boundaries),

  6–12/14/17/33/44, 13/15/19/33/36, 20/21/34, 23–25, and the
  reorganization obligations 29/37. Each row above identifies its own
  precondition, postcondition, and distinct killed mutant.
